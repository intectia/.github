# Intectia

**Native mobile engineering, built to last.**

We are senior iOS and Android engineers who build, modernize and scale native apps in
Swift, SwiftUI, Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. No cross-platform shortcuts, no handoff to
juniors. The engineers you meet on the first call are the ones who ship, and they stay
to scale what they built.

### What we do
- **iOS** — Swift and SwiftUI, StoreKit, widgets, Live Activities, App Store review done properly
- **Android** — Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, Material 3, Play Console, device fragmentation handled
- **Modernization and rescue** — migrations off React Native and Flutter, Objective-C and Java rewrites, stabilizing apps that crash in production
- **Embedded senior engineers** — one to five native engineers inside your team, on your tools and your process

### How we work
1. **Technical discovery** — a senior engineer reviews your codebase, roadmap and constraints and writes an assessment within a week
2. **A named team** — you meet the engineers before you sign
3. **Working software every two weeks** — TestFlight and internal-track builds every sprint, with release notes a product manager can read

### Track record
- 30+ native apps shipped to the App Store and Google Play
- 10+ years of native experience per engineer
- 4.6 average store rating across live apps
- 80% of clients still with us after year one
- Apps shipped for Fortune 500 and Inc. 5000 companies, and apps featured at WWDC
